# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This project exists to develop an evidence-governed standard for interpretive integrity. Participation must therefore preserve the same distinction the standard requires: critique claims and artifacts without inventing motives or psychological explanations for contributors.

## Expected conduct

Participants are expected to:

- address the actual proposal, evidence, implementation, or test;
- distinguish fact, inference, opinion, and unresolved uncertainty;
- correct misquotation or scope substitution directly;
- acknowledge valid corrections and integrate them in later discussion;
- preserve safety, privacy, and legal boundaries;
- disagree without harassment, threats, or personal degradation.

## Unacceptable conduct

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory abuse;
- publishing private information without permission;
- repeated misrepresentation after correction;
- assigning motives, diagnoses, or character judgments as substitutes for technical review;
- using project channels to pressure maintainers into unsupported claims or false concessions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, lock, or restrict participation where necessary to protect contributors and the integrity of the project. Enforcement decisions should identify the conduct at issue and preserve an auditable record where appropriate.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security or maintainer contact path rather than escalating personal disputes in public threads.
